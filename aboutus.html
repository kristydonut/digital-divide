About Us

At Cipher, we believe that digital literacy is more than just a skill—it's a key to inclusion in today's fast-paced world. The name Cipher reflects both sides of the digital divide: those who can "decode" and thrive in navigating digital spaces, and those left at "zero," excluded due to limited access or knowledge.

Our mission is to bridge this gap by sharing stories, resources, and opportunities that empower individuals to step confidently into the digital age. Through awareness, education, and connection, Cipher works to ensure that no one is left behind in the evolving digital landscape.
