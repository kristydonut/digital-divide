The Problem of Internet Access

The digital divide represents one of the most pressing challenges of our time, creating stark inequalities between those who have reliable internet access and those who don't.

Urban Areas

  • High-speed fiber connections
  • Multiple internet service providers
  • Competitive pricing
  • 5G network coverage
  • Digital infrastructure investment
  • Tech support availability

Rural Areas

  • Limited or no broadband access
  • Few service provider options
  • Higher costs for basic service
  • Poor mobile network coverage
  • Aging infrastructure
  • Limited technical support

Impact Across Different Fields

Digital divide simply leads to the question of social inequality (Alvarez, 2021). Depriving the citizen to have access to information creates gaps, and these information gaps result in the dehumanization of individual rights (Alvarez, 2021).
