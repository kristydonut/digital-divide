What is Digital Divide?

Digital divide refers to the division between people who have access and use of digital media and those who do not. It is the gap that was created with the unequal access to modern information and communication technologies (ICTs). The term was first used to describe the gap of accessibility on computers and the internet but it has since expanded to measure the divide to be a multifaceted issue including factors such as affordability, quality, and relevance. It has caused socioeconomic inequalities and affected opportunities in education, work, and daily life.
